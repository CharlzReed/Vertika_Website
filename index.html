Process

From artwork to finished wall

The process is simple. We prepare the file, calibrate the printer to the surface and print the image directly on site.

Step 03

Print directly

About

Driven by Innovation, Defined by Precision

At 20 years old, my world exists at the intersection of complex problem-solving and entrepreneurial drive. Balancing double-track studies in both Engineering and Pre-Medical Sciences, I’ve built a mindset grounded in rigorous technical accuracy, relentless discipline, and an obsession with how systems work—from human biology to state-of-the-art mechanical design.

Vertika was born out of a desire to bridge high-tech robotics with interior architecture. Where traditional paint and wallpaper fall short with messy installations and design limits, vertical wall printing offers endless possibilities. Drawing on my background in engineering logic and medical-grade attention to detail, I bring a precise, tech-forward standard to every wall we transform—ensuring crisp, high-definition art that turns blank vertical spaces into striking visual statements.

Engineering Precision

Applying mathematical accuracy and exact calibration for flawless high-resolution prints.

Medical-Grade Detail

Approaching every site assessment, ceiling limitation, and surface texture with meticulous care.

Entrepreneurial Drive

Built on a relentless work ethic and a mission to revolutionize interior design through robotics.
