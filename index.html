Process

From artwork to finished wall

The process is simple. We prepare the file, calibrate the printer to the surface and print the image directly on site.

Step 01

Prepare the artwork

We review your image and prepare it for the dimensions, scale and layout of the wall.

Step 02

Set up on site

The printer is positioned and calibrated to the wall before printing begins.

Step 03

Print directly

The image is printed onto the wall and cured with UV light during the printing process.
